# Riot answer — ticket limits and multi-event carts (received 2026-10-08)

Answer from Andrew (Riot Games), relayed by the product team on 2026-10-08. Verbatim.

Side events should be limited to 1 per person as well, but honestly it should be configurable on the backend how many tickets of a ticket type you should be able to purchase. I say this because in the future, on demand events will allow you to buy multiple tickets.

Buying tickets across multiple events will not happen within just Riftbound, as events almost never overlap. However, once we have multiple sports we may want to allow for multiple tickets in the cart across events. But again, that needs to just be configurable from the backend. It's surprising to me that we can't limit ticket quantities per ticket type on the backend side. Is that not a feature that is available? It feels tablestakes to a ticketing platform…
